# Open questions

Items that need a decision by the project owner or confirmation by a firefighting expert. Nothing
here has been decided silently: where the simulator needed a value or an interpretation, it is
marked `derived` / `assumption` with `review.status: requires-review` and the question is listed
below with the place where it was applied.

References: *Vj1* = `content/source/01-Vjezba-1.md`, *Vj2* = `content/source/02-Vjezba-2.md`,
line numbers in parentheses; step ids refer to the authoring files in `content/authoring/source/`.

## Materials

**Q1. Are the SVG symbols and sketches faithful to the printed vježbovnik?** All SVGs carry a
C2PA note that an AI assistant created or modified them, and they are generated by `alati/*.py`
(SOURCE_ANALYSIS.md). Positions, proportions and which items are drawn should be checked against
the printed figures before the exercises are marked `verified`.

**Q2. Formation drills** (`00-3-Postrojavanje.md`, sketches 1–9) are analysed but not modelled. Should
they become exercises? They contain distances (*rastojanje 0,10 m*, *odstojanje 1,10 m*) that could
give the scene a real scale.

## Vježba 1 — interpretations to confirm

8. **Initial formation**: the simulation starts with the squad in formation as in sketch 11; the
   movement into formation after „Odjeljenje zbor!” (Vj1 29) is not shown because positions before
   the order are not given.
9. **„istupa pet koraka naprijed”** (Vj1 35; Vj2 30 likewise): the sketches have no scale, so the
   five steps forward are not shown — only the turn (*čelom nazad*). Which distance should a step be?
10. **When does the commander go to the distributor?** *„Nakon izdane zapovijedi, zapovjednik odlazi
    do kraja „B” cijevnog voda…”* (Vj1 63) — after the main order or after „Vodu daj!”? Modelled
    after the distributor has been placed (step `zapovjednik-razdjelnica`), since he stands *desno
    od razdjelnice*.
11. **Parallel work**: the navalna grupa's equipping (Vj1 55–57) is modelled in parallel with laying
    the „B” line (it depends only on the main order); walking to the end of the line waits for the
    line (Vj1 59). Is this the intended order?
12. **N₁ picks up his own „C” coil again** before laying the first jet (Vj1 81 *„skida nosač svoje
    „C” cijevi”*); he put it on the ground in Vj1 59. The pick-up is implied, not stated.
13. **Second jet valve**: for the first jet the text says Z opens the left valve (Vj1 81); for the
    second jet it only says V₁ orders „Za drugi mlaz vodu daj!” and receives the acknowledgement
    (Vj1 99–101). Opening the right valve is **not** modelled. In pospremanje Z closes *ventile*
    (plural, Vj1 113). Should the right valve be opened?
14. **Spare „C” coils**: sketch 13 draws N₂'s spare coil right of the distributor (636,400), sketch 14
    draws two spare coils at (586,395) and (586,413). Modelled: N₂'s at the sketch 13 place, V₂'s at
    the sketch 14 place.
15. **Strojar returns the nastavak and key** to the staging area: shown only in sketch 15; the text
    (Vj1 117) does not say where he puts them. The same holds for the commander's and strojar's
    places *u stroju* (modelled as their initial places in sketch 11, because sketch 15 uses a
    different spacing).
16. **Nozzles and holders** stay in the belt (*za opasače*, Vj1 119) and are put back at the end
    because Vj1 154 says *„sva oprema odložena na svoje mjesto”*. Confirm.
17. **Which hose each member rolls up** (Vj1 119–129): modelled as the leader rolling the outer hose
    (nozzle end) and the member the inner one (from the coupling he separated). Where the coil ends
    up is schematic (at the hose's first end).
18. **Movement rule** *„članovi grupa idu s lijeve strane svojih vođa grupe”* (Vj1 148) is applied in
    the demonstration but not checked in training.
19. **Positions in the demonstration** that no sketch shows (where a person stands while taking or
    handing over equipment, intermediate walking points) are schematic and written directly into
    the scripts; positions shown on sketches are referenced with `$diagramPoint`.

    Vj2 24; the strojar *„predaje prijavak vođi navalne grupe”*, Vj2 70). Modelled as actions without

## Simulation choices (technical, not doctrine)

| Setting / choice | Value | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pickUpDistance` / `placeDistance` | 70 / 90 units | Proximity is only schematic (no scale). Acceptable? |
| `connectionMaxDistance` | 100 units | Ports must be near each other to connect. Acceptable? |
| `maxCarriedPerPerson` | none | The text has people carry up to four items (hands + belt). Limit? |
| `orderPolicy` | `warn` | Should out-of-order actions be rejected in assessment? |
| Roles | procedural actions (orders, signals, valves) accept only the roles the text names; spatial actions (carrying, connecting, laying) accept anyone | Should all actions be restricted to the roles in the text? |
| Laid hoses | straight line between both ends | Draw curves/arcs? |
| Carried items | drawn at half size beside the carrier | — |
| Assessment criteria | none (report only, `passed: null`) | Which criteria and limits should an assessment use? |
