# Open questions

Items that need a decision by the project owner or confirmation by a firefighting expert. Nothing
here has been decided silently: where the simulator needed a value or an interpretation, it is
marked `derived` / `assumption` with `review.status: requires-review` and the question is listed
below with the place where it was applied.

References: *Vj1* = `content/source/01-Vjezba-1.md`, *Vj2* = `content/source/02-Vjezba-2.md`,
line numbers in parentheses; step ids refer to the authoring files in `content/authoring/source/`.

## Materials

**Q1. Are the SVG symbols and sketches faithful to the printed vježbovnik?** All SVGs carry a
C2PA note that an AI assistant created or modified them, and they are generated by `alati/*.py`
(SOURCE_ANALYSIS.md). Positions, proportions and which items are drawn should be checked against
the printed figures before the exercises are marked `verified`.

**Q2. Formation drills** (`00-3-Postrojavanje.md`, sketches 1–9) are analysed but not modelled. Should
they become exercises? They contain distances (*rastojanje 0,10 m*, *odstojanje 1,10 m*) that could
give the scene a real scale.

## Vježba 3 — usisni vod (Vj3 = `content/source/03-Vjezba-3.md`)

## Demonstration sound

26. **Resolved orders**: Vj1 111 quotes *„Prvi, odnosno drugi mlaz – vodom stoj!”* for both leaders;
    the demonstration has N₁ say „Prvi mlaz – vodom stoj!” and V₁ „Drugi mlaz – vodom stoj!”
    (`audio.speech-not-verbatim`). Correct?
27. **Alternatives**: for *„Na mjestu odmor!” ili „Otpust!”* (Vj1 160) the demonstration speaks the first.
28. **No wording in the source, no speech**: the formation order and the strojar's report in Vježba 2,
    and hand signals (*daje znak*), have no spoken audio.
29. **Pronunciation** of the synthesized Croatian (abbreviations N₁/V₂, „B”/„C”, the placeholder
    *pravac ……*) should be checked by listening before the clips are published.
54. **Shared paragraphs**: where several steps come from one paragraph (*„kao u vježbi 1”*), the
    paragraph is narrated once, at the first of them, and the others only play their orders. An order
    quoted in such a paragraph is then read by the narrator in the first step and spoken again by the
    order voice in the step that performs it (e.g. Vježba 2, *„Za prvi mlaz…”*).

## Simulation choices (technical, not doctrine)

| Setting / choice | Value | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pickUpDistance` / `placeDistance` | 70 / 90 units | Proximity is only schematic (no scale). Acceptable? |
| `connectionMaxDistance` | 100 units | Ports must be near each other to connect. Acceptable? |
| `maxCarriedPerPerson` | none | The text has people carry up to four items (hands + belt). Limit? |
| `orderPolicy` | `warn` | Should out-of-order actions be rejected in assessment? |
| Roles | procedural actions (orders, signals, valves) accept only the roles the text names; spatial actions (carrying, connecting, laying) accept anyone | Should all actions be restricted to the roles in the text? |
| Laid hoses | straight line between both ends | Draw curves/arcs? |
| Carried items | drawn at half size beside the carrier | — |
| Assessment criteria | none (report only, `passed: null`) | Which criteria and limits should an assessment use? |
